Placeholders
  • {message} — the alert as one line (Slack).
  • {subject}, {body} — the email's own subject and text.
  • {time} — when it was sent; {panel} — the panel's address; {kind} — what kind of alert it is, such as backup.
  • A placeholder Hyperion does not know is sent as typed, so a typo shows.
  • What the preview shows is what goes out, and what the Email log records.
  • The sentences of the alerts themselves are reworded with the customer letters, in Sentences.
