{# Snapshots. Lazy-loaded over HTMX from the Backups tab. A snapshot is not another copy of the site — restic stores each chunk of content once, so thirty of them cost a little more than one. What they buy that a tar.gz cannot is the diff: after a site breaks following an update, "what actually changed?" has an answer. Failures render INSIDE the card via `error`: HTMX does not swap on a non-2xx response, so an error status leaves a spinner on screen. Every form that changes something is a plain POST that starts a job, like the archive actions beside it: the jobs panel on the tab shows it running, and a dropped connection does not stop it. #}
