Choosing a relay
  • Use a real provider — Postmark, Mailgun, SendGrid, Brevo, Amazon SES, Gmail SMTP — or a postfix of your own that relays outbound. Mail sent straight from a VPS to public mailboxes almost always lands in spam.
  • SPF — the receiving server checks that the relay is on the permitted-sender list (a DNS TXT record) of the sender address's domain. Each hosting's page shows its own SPF analysis.
  • Sender address — on a domain you control whose SPF includes the relay. A placeholder on somebody else's domain is accepted and then silently discarded by the receiver.
    • {% if is_master %}
  • Detect a local relay checks localhost:25 and :587 on this server for a postfix or exim that already accepts mail, and fills the form; the sender address it suggests comes from /etc/mailname. Nothing is saved until you press Save.
    • {% endif %}
  • Every send is logged in the Email log with the relay's full answer, so "accepted" (250) and "refused" (550) are told apart without leaving the panel.
