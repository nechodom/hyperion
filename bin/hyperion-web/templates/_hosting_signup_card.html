{# Public WordPress registration — the actual door. This card exists because of a real incident: a site took six spam sign-ups a minute and the operator's first thought was that fail2ban had broken. It had not. fail2ban counts repeated FAILURES from one address; a registration flood is many addresses making one SUCCESSFUL request each, so there is nothing for it to count and nothing for it to ban. The only thing that stops it is WordPress not creating the account. Lazily swapped in over HTMX, because answering it costs a wp-cli call on the owning node. #}
